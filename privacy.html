Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

"Quiet Player Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Quiet Player. It is not offered to the public and no other users sign in to it.

What the tool accesses

Through Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, the tool accesses only the channel owner's own YouTube account in order to: upload the channel's videos and thumbnails, edit video and channel metadata, post comments on the channel's own videos, and read the channel's own performance statistics.

How data is stored and shared

Access tokens and analytics reports are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The tool does not collect any data about viewers beyond the aggregated, anonymous statistics YouTube Analytics provides to channel owners.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's own terms apply: YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel through youtube.com/@TheQuietPlayer.
